# Design rules

- No AI-style icons anywhere, ever: no sparkles (lucide `Sparkles`, `Sparkle`), magic wands (`Wand`,
  `Wand2`, `WandSparkles`), or drawn four-point sparkle shapes, in icons, SVG drawings or emoji (✨).
  Pick an icon that says what the thing does (a lightbulb for tips, a link for magic links, a trophy
  for XP).
